\section{Risk indicators}

In this section, we will first examine the theory surrounding risk indicators known as \textbf{Greeks}, and then use the binomial model to approximate them. We will limit our scope to the primary Greeks: $\Delta, \Gamma, \nu, \Theta$. While many others exist—such as $\rho, \lambda, \epsilon$—they are less relevant given the assumptions underlying the binomial model (constant risk-free rate, no dividends, etc.).
